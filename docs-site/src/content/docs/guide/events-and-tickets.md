If your site lists events, they appear under **Content**, on the **Events** tab, and on your site's
calendar at `/events/`. (If you don't see an **Events** tab, events are turned off; an admin can turn
them on under **Settings**, **Events**.)

## Adding an event

1. Click **Content**, then **Events**, then **New Event**.
2. Give it a **Title** in the right-hand panel.
3. Set **Starts** and **Ends**. Tick **All-day event** if it has no set times.
4. Pick the **Venue**. If it is a new place, add it right there: type the name and fill in the address.
5. Describe the event on the page itself, as with any page.
6. Add a **Featured image**: it is used on the calendar and in event lists.
7. Click **Publish**.

The event appears on the calendar straight away, and visitors can add it to their own calendars.

## Events that repeat

For a weekly class or a monthly meeting, use **Repeats** in the event's settings. Choose **Daily**,
**Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then when it **Ends**: **On a date**, **After a number of
times**, or **Never** (the calendar then shows two years ahead). **Skip dates** leaves
out particular days (a holiday, say), and **Extra dates** adds one-off dates. Each date gets its own
page on the site.

## Venues

Venues are kept once and reused. Find them under **Content**, **Venues** to correct an address or
phone number; every event at that venue updates.

## Selling tickets or taking sign-ups

An event takes bookings once it has at least one ticket type. In the event's settings, under
**Tickets**:

1. Add a ticket type, for example "Adult" or "Free entry", with its price (0 for free).
2. Optionally set **How many** are available, and when it is **On sale from** and **until**.
3. Set the event's overall capacity if there is one, and whether to offer a **Waitlist when sold
   out**.
4. Publish the event. The booking form appears on its page.

Paid tickets need card payments set up for your site; ask your admin. Free tickets work without.

## Who is coming

Click **People**, then **Tickets**, in the top bar to see every event taking bookings, with tickets sold and takings.
Click an event to see its **Orders**:

- **Checked in**: tick people off at the door. **Print** gives you a paper list.
- **Resend confirmation**: if someone lost their email.
- **Cancel order** or **Refund** (refunds are made by admins).
- **Export CSV**: the list as a spreadsheet.
- **Waitlist**: the people waiting, and **Notify** to tell them tickets are free.
- **Emails**: change the confirmation and reminder emails for this event.
