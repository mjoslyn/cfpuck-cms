If your site lists events, they appear under **Content**, **Events**, and on your site's
calendar at `/events/`. (If you don't see **Events** under **Content**, events are turned off; an admin can turn
them on under **Settings**, **Events**.)

## Adding an event

1. Click **Content**, then **Events**, then **New Event**.
2. Give it a **Title** in the right-hand panel.
3. Set **Starts** and **Ends**. Tick **All-day event** if it has no set times.
4. Pick the **Venue**. If it is a new place, add it right there: type the name and fill in the address.
5. Describe the event on the page itself, as with any page.
6. Add a **Featured image**: it is used on the calendar and in event lists.
7. Click **Publish**.

The event appears on the calendar straight away, and visitors can add it to their own calendars.

## Events that repeat

For a weekly class or a monthly meeting, use **Repeats** in the event's settings. Choose **Daily**,
**Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then when it **Ends**: **On a date**, **After a number of
times**, or **Never** (the calendar then shows two years ahead). **Skip dates** leaves
out particular days (a holiday, say), and **Extra dates** adds one-off dates. Each date gets its own
page on the site.

## Venues

Venues are kept once and reused. Find them under **Content**, **Venues** to correct an address or
phone number; every event at that venue updates.

## Selling tickets or taking sign-ups

Tickets are made under **People** > **Tickets**, and an event sells the ticket it is attached to. One
ticket can be sold at several events, from one pool, like a festival pass.

The quickest way: in the event's settings, under **Tickets**, choose **New ticket for this event…** in
**Sells**, give the ticket a name and press **Make**. The ticket opens in a new tab. If another ticket
already has that name, a number is added (for example "Christmas Stroll 2"). Or click **People**, then **Tickets**, type a name next to
**New ticket** and press **Make ticket**. Then, under **Ticket types and settings**:

1. Add a ticket type, for example "Adult" or "Free entry", with its price (0 for free).
2. Optionally set **How many** are available, and when it is **On sale from** and **until**. Leave
   **How many** empty for no limit: 0 means none left, and the tickets show as sold out. The ticket
   type's row shows how many are available while it is closed.
3. Set the overall capacity if there is one, and whether to offer a **Waitlist when sold out**.
4. Press **Save tickets**.
5. If you made the ticket from the Tickets list, attach the event: under **Events selling this
   ticket**, choose it and press **Attach**. (Or choose the ticket in the event's settings, under
   **Sells**.)

Publish the event. The booking form appears on its page, under the event's description, whatever
template the event uses. To put it somewhere else on the page, add a **Tickets** block there.

To sell a ticket on any other page (a home page, or a "What's on" page), add a **Tickets** block and
choose the ticket under **Ticket** in its settings. For a ticket sold at several events, the block lists
them under the ticket's name. For a ticket of one repeating event, **Date** picks the date (left empty,
the next one). **Manage ticket** opens the ticket's setup in a new tab.

### One ticket for several events

Attach more events to the same ticket (on the ticket's page, or in each event's settings under
**Sells**). Tickets bought at any of them count against the same ticket types and capacity. The
ticket's **Orders** list shows everyone, with the event each person booked at; choose an event at the
top to see only its bookings. **Detach** stops an event selling the ticket; its orders stay.

Paid tickets need card payments set up for your site; ask your admin. Free tickets work without.

## Who is coming

Click **People**, then **Tickets**, in the top bar to see every ticket, the events selling it, tickets
sold and takings. Click a ticket to see its **Orders**:

- **Checked in**: tick people off at the door. **Print** gives you a paper list.
- **Resend confirmation**: if someone lost their email.
- **Cancel order** or **Refund** (refunds are made by admins).
- **Export CSV**: the list as a spreadsheet.
- **Waitlist**: the people waiting, and **Notify** to tell them tickets are free.
- **Emails**: change the confirmation and reminder emails for this ticket.
