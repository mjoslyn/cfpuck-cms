All your images live in **Media**, in the top bar. They can be used on any page.

![The Media library](../../../assets/guide/media.png)

## Uploading an image

**From the Media screen:**

1. Click **Media** in the top bar.
2. Click **Choose File** and pick the image on your computer.
3. Click **Upload**.

**From the page editor:** wherever you can add an image (an **Image** block, a **Featured image**, a
**Hero**), click **Choose**. You can pick one already in the library or upload a new one there and
then.

:::tip
Use photos at least 1600 pixels wide for big banner pictures. The site makes smaller copies for
phones by itself, so you don't need to resize anything.
:::

## Describing an image (alt text)

Click an image in **Media** to open its details.

![An image's details: alt text on the right, the focal point on the left](../../../assets/guide/media-detail.png)

**Alt text** is a short description of the picture, read aloud to people who can't see it and shown
if the picture doesn't load. Write what someone would need to know, for example "Children planting
flowers at the community garden". Leave it empty only for pictures that are purely decoration.

**Not sure what to write?** Click **Generate** beside the box. Claude looks at the picture and writes
a description for you. Read it and change anything that isn't right (it can't know people's names
unless the title or caption says them), then click **Save**. Adding a title or caption first gives it
more to go on.

**Title** and **Caption** are optional. Click **Save** when done.

## Keeping the important part in view

Pictures are often cropped to fit, for example into a square on a list of posts. To choose which part
always stays in view, **click on the most important part of the picture** (a face, a building).
The small previews underneath show how each crop will look.

Under each preview is its shape (for example **16:9**, wide like a TV screen) and the smallest picture
that fills it sharply, such as **1600 × 900 or larger**. If it says **this one is smaller**, that crop may
look a little blurry on big screens: use a larger photo, or **Use another image** for that shape.

If one crop still doesn't work, for example a wide photo that looks wrong as a square, click **Use
another image** under that preview and pick a different picture to use only for that shape.

### Focal points for one block

The **Hero**, **Cover** and (when cropped) **Image** blocks can have their own focal point for each
screen size. Select the block, then under **Focal point** pick **Desktop**, **Tablet** or **Phone** and
click the picture. The small preview beside it shows how it will be cropped on that screen. A size you
don't set uses the picture's own focal point from **Media**; **Use the image's own** goes back to it.
Tip: in the editor, the phone and tablet buttons above the page show it at that size.

## Finding an image

Type in the search box on the **Media** screen. It searches file names and alt text, which is another
good reason to write alt text.

## Videos and documents

- **Videos from YouTube or Vimeo**: add an **Embed** or video block to the page and paste the video's
  web address.
- **Video files**: upload them like images. Large videos take a while to upload.

## Documents (PDFs, Word, Excel...)

You can upload documents to **Media** just like pictures: PDFs, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files,
OpenDocument files, plain text and CSV, up to 50 MB each. You can choose several files at once.

Then link to them from a page in any of these ways:

- **The File block** (in **Blocks**, under Media): shows the document's name, its type and size, and a
  **Download** button. Choose the file in its settings; you can change the link's words and the button.
- **From some words in a paragraph**: select the words, then click **File** above the text box and pick
  the document.
- **From a button** (or an image or hero): click **File** beside its **Link** box and pick the document.

To put a document's address in an email or a newsletter, open it in **Media** and click **Copy link**.
Every picture has **Copy link** too.

:::tip
PDFs open in the visitor's browser. Word, Excel and PowerPoint files download.
:::

:::note
Images are never deleted from the library, so a picture used on an old page can't go missing by
accident.
:::
