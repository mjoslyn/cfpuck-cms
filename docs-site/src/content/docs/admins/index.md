This guide is for admins: the people who set a site up and decide how it works. Admins can do
everything editors can, so for pages, images, forms and publishing, see the
[Editor guide](/editors/). This guide covers what only admins can change.

The **?** button at the top of every admin screen brings admins here, and editors to the Editor guide.

## What only admins see

- **Settings**: the gear ⚙ at the right of the top bar. Everything that applies to the whole site:
  its name, the menu, which features are on, connected services, redirects and backups.
- **People**, **Users**: who can sign in to look after the site, and whether they are an editor or an
  admin. **Members** too, when the site has members.
- Links in the page editor to edit the header, footer and other shared parts a page uses.
