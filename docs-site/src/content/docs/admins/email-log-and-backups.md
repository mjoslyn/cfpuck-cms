## Email log

To check whether an email went out, turn on **Keep a log of the emails the site sends** in **Settings >
Email log**, choose how many days to keep them, and press **Save settings**. Then **Open the email log**
lists each email: when, who it went to, its subject and whether it was sent. Click a subject to see the
email as it was sent.

Emails with a sign-in, password, invitation or confirmation link show only who they went to and their
subject, so nobody reading the log can use the link. Mailing list emails aren't in the log. Turning the
log off deletes what it kept within the hour.

## Backups

The site can back itself up every day (**Settings > Backups**). Each backup can be restored if something
goes badly wrong. Restoring brings back pages and settings, not images, which are never deleted
anyway. If you're unsure, ask whoever set up your site before restoring.
